# Frequently asked questions

**Who this is for:** anyone looking for a quick answer.

## For everyone

**Is Cubhouse a social network?**
No. It is one family's private site. Nobody outside the family can see it, search engines are told
to stay away, and there are no ads or trackers.

**Who can see the photos?**
The guardians always can. Everyone else sees only what the guardians share with them. See
[What you can and cannot see](family-members.md#what-you-can-and-cannot-see).

**Why does the app say "Parent" when this guide says "guardian"?**
They mean the same thing: the child's parents or guardians, who run the site.

**Do I need two-step verification?**
Guardians must use it. Family members can choose, and we recommend it. See
[Getting started](getting-started.md#two-step-verification).

**Why was I signed out?**
After 15 minutes of doing nothing, or 7 days in any case. Changing your password or using
**Sign out everywhere** on another device also signs you out.

**Is there a phone app?**
No. Use your phone's web browser. You can add the site to your home screen from the browser's
share menu.

**Can I change my name or email?**
Yes, in **Settings**, **Profile**. Changing your email needs your current password.

## For family members

**Can I share a photo with someone outside the family?**
Please do not. Downloaded copies are traced back to who downloaded them.

**Why can I download some photos but not others?**
The guardians decide for each photo. If there is no **Download** button, it is view only.

**Can other people see my albums?**
No. Albums are only yours.

**Can I post my own photos?**
Yes. Use **Add a photo** on **Home**. A guardian approves it first. See
[Send in a photo](family-members.md#send-in-a-photo).

**A photo disappeared. What happened?**
A guardian changed who can see it, hid it, or deleted it. It also disappears from your albums.

**Why can I not comment?**
Either comments are off for that photo ("Comments are turned off for this photo"), or a guardian
paused commenting on your account ("Commenting is paused on your account").

**Can I report a photo?**
There is a **Report** button for comments only. For a photo, tell a guardian directly.

**How do I leave?**
**Settings**, **Delete account**. See [Data export and erasure](data-export-and-erasure.md#delete-your-account).

## For guardians

**Why is my change "waiting for approval"?**
It widens who can see or keep photos, invites someone, or deletes a lot. The other guardian must
approve. See [Two-guardian approval](two-guardian-governance.md).

**How do I stop someone seeing photos right now?**
**Admin**, **Members**, **Remove and revoke**. They are signed out at once. Hiding a photo or
locking downloads is also instant.

**Can the other guardian undo my "restrict" changes alone?**
No. Widening again needs your approval.

**Someone lost their access code. What now?**
Revoke the old one in **Invite codes** and request a new one.

**What does the visible mark look like?**
A band at the bottom saying "Private family photo. Please do not share. Ref ..." and a faint
reference across the middle. See [Download a photo](family-members.md#download-a-photo).

**Are my own downloads marked?**
No. Guardians' downloads are recorded but not marked.

**Can I see who looked at a photo?**
No. Only downloads are recorded, not views.
